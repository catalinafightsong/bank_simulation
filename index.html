Lesson 1
Keep it simple

Simple systems are safer

The more steps and systems you use, the more chances there are for mistakes. Fewer steps are easier to check.

Lesson 2
Use facts

Use facts to choose protection

Use costs and likely losses to decide whether a security measure is worth paying for. Do not rely on scare tactics.

Lesson 3
Work together

People, rules, and tools work together

A security tool cannot fix everything by itself. Staff and clear rules matter too.

Lesson 4
